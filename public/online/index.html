Before you visit

Where are the two shops?

Flushing is inside Golden Shopping Mall at 41-28 Main St, Unit T6, Flushing, NY 11355. FiDi is on the lower level of Golden Mall FiDi at 47 Broadway, Unit C7, New York, NY 10006.

Can I order ahead for pickup?

Yes. Both shops take pickup orders on Chowbus. Choose Flushing or FiDi above, or use Order online at the top of the page.

Do you deliver?

Flushing delivers through Uber Eats, DoorDash and Grubhub. FiDi delivers through DoorDash and Grubhub.

Can I ask about ingredients or allergens?

Please call your shop before ordering so the team can help with your specific product and dietary questions.
